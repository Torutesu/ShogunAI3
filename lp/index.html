FAQ

Asked, answered.

Is SHOGUN taking screenshots of my screen?

No. SHOGUN reads on-screen text through the macOS Accessibility API. There is no screenshot, video or OCR pipeline in the app. Secure fields, private browsing and payment pages are filtered out before storage.

Where is my memory stored?

In a local database on your Mac. Search, ranking and deletion all run on-device. Optional cloud sync is off by default and end-to-end encrypted when enabled.

What does BYOK mean here?

Bring Your Own Key. SHOGUN calls the model provider you configure — OpenAI, Anthropic or Gemini-compatible — with your key. Your content goes to your provider, never to us.

What machines does it run on?

macOS on Apple Silicon. The beta ships as an unsigned build: on first launch, right-click the app and choose Open (macOS 15+: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway).

Can I really delete everything?

Yes. Delete-by-range removes both indexed memory and the raw captured text behind it. Delete-all wipes the local database and clears every key from the Keychain.
