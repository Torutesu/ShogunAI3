# SHOGUN Waiting List Referral Program — Official Rules

Last updated: July 17, 2026

These rules govern the SHOGUN waiting list referral program (the
"Program"), operated by Select KK ("Sponsor", "we"). By joining the
waiting list or sharing a referral link, you agree to these rules.

**No purchase is necessary to participate or to earn any reward. The
Program involves no element of chance: every reward is determined solely
by verifiable participant actions.**

## 1. Eligibility

- You must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where you
  live, if higher).
- One waiting list entry and one referral link per person. Entries created
  with automated tools, disposable identities operated by the same person,
  or on behalf of others are not eligible.
- The Program is void where prohibited by law.
- Employees and contractors of Sponsor may join the waiting list but are
  not eligible for rewards.

## 2. How the Program works

1. Join the waiting list at syogun.com with your email address. You
   receive a personal referral link.
2. A **qualified invite** is counted when a new person (a) joins the
   waiting list through your link with an email address not previously
   registered, and (b) completes the three signup questions on their
   status page.
3. Your queue position and reward tier update as qualified invites
   accumulate. Counts are determined by Sponsor's records, which are
   final.

## 3. Rewards

| Qualified invites | Reward |
|---|---|
| 3 | 1 month of SHOGUN subscription, free |
| 10 | 3 months of SHOGUN subscription, free |
| 30 | 6 months of SHOGUN subscription, free |
| Top 10 referrers at Program close | 12 months of SHOGUN subscription, free |

- **Rewards replace, they do not stack.** You receive the single highest
  tier you reach. Reaching 10 invites replaces the 1-month reward with the
  3-month reward; it does not add to it.
- The top-10 ranking is determined once, when the Program closes, by
  qualified invite count; ties are broken by earlier waiting list signup.
  Sponsor will announce the close date in advance.
- Rewards are applied to your SHOGUN account as free subscription time
  starting at general availability. Use of SHOGUN requires a supported
  device and, for AI features, your own model-provider key; provider usage
  fees are yours.
- Rewards are personal, non-transferable, cannot be sold or exchanged,
  and have no cash value. They cannot be redeemed for cash, credit, or
  any other asset.
- If you already hold a paid subscription when a reward activates, the
  free period is appended to your current billing period.

## 4. Fair play

The following void the related invites and may disqualify the participant
entirely, at Sponsor's reasonable discretion:

- Self-referral in any form, including inviting addresses you control.
- Automated, scripted, or purchased signups; incentivizing strangers to
  sign up with payment or deception.
- Spam. Sharing your link is welcome; bulk unsolicited messaging is not,
  and may also violate applicable law.
- Operating multiple entries per person or circumventing platform rules
  of any social network, including prompting repeated reposts of the same
  post.

If you promote your link in a post for which you receive or expect any
payment or reward beyond this Program's published tiers, you must clearly
disclose the material connection (for example `#ad`) as required by the
FTC Endorsement Guides and equivalent laws in your country. Organic,
uncompensated sharing needs no disclosure.

## 5. Your data

We collect your email address and, optionally, your answers to the three
signup questions. We use them to order the waiting list, operate the
Program, and contact you about SHOGUN access and launch. Referral counts
shown publicly are displayed against a masked form of your email address.
See our Privacy Policy for your rights, including access and deletion:
https://github.com/Torutesu/ShogunAI3/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

Deleting your waiting list entry removes you from the queue and forfeits
unactivated rewards.

## 6. Changes and termination

Sponsor may amend these rules or suspend or end the Program at any time
for legal, technical, or abuse-related reasons. Reward tiers already
reached with qualified invites before such a change are honored. The
waiting list confers no right to receive access to SHOGUN at any
particular time.
